## Recommendation

"Mute" by Matrix (Jamie Quinn) is a Prototype Recordings (Grooverider's label) drum & bass track. The original "Mute" first appeared on the 1997 compilation "Grooverider Presents The Prototype Years" and IS available streaming on Spotify (album https://open.spotify.com/album/7ewM0AVaBniZo7cGKssDBm) - best route for a clean copy of the original. A reworked "Mute '98" came out as a vinyl-only 12" in 1998 (Prototype PRO 011, b/w "Convoy") and has no official digital release - if the radio mix used that 1998 version you'd need to buy and rip the 12" (available used on Discogs ~from a few GBP). Recommend matching the radio rip to the Spotify 1997 version first; if it's the harder/spacier '98 rework, source the vinyl.
