## Recommendation

"Your Sound" by J Majik is a 1995 drum & bass classic, originally released as the A-side of the 12" "Your Sound / Tranquil" on Metalheadz (cat# METH 006 / MET007, 1995). The original mix is officially available as a lossless digital download (FLAC, 16-bit/44.1kHz) for ~GBP 4 from Infrared Records' Bandcamp, which lists the exact original tracks ("Your Sound" 6:11, "Tranquil" 5:48) dated January 7, 1995. Buy the Bandcamp FLAC for a clean, lossless copy of this exact version; avoid the later "Your Sound (Remix)" / "Your Sound Remixes" releases on Infrared/Beatport, which are different versions.
