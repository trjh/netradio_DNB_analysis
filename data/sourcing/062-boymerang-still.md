## Recommendation

"Still" by Boymerang (Graham Sutton) first appeared as the A-side of the 1996 12" Still / Urban Space on Prototype Recordings (cat# PRO 008), then was re-issued as a track on Boymerang's 1997 album Balance Of The Force (Regal Recordings / EMI, cat# REG 13). The album is the version that is digitally distributed today and matches the Spotify lead. Best acquisition: buy the lossless album track digitally (iTunes/Apple Music, Bandcamp/Beatport listings of Balance Of The Force) rather than ripping vinyl. If the original Prototype mix is specifically wanted, the 1996 12" is vinyl-only and would need a rip; note there is also a later "Still (V.I.P. Mix)" on a Boymerang / Dom & Roland split that is a different version.
