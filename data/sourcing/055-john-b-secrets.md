## Recommendation

"Secrets" by John B was a compilation-exclusive track that first appeared on Grooverider Presents: The Prototype Years (Prototype Recordings, 1997) - it was never issued as a standalone single, only later getting a "Secrets (Remix)" on John B's Visions LP (1998). The exact original version is on streaming now: the full comp is on Spotify and Apple Music, so that is the easiest clean source. For lossless, buy the 1997 2xCD (cat# 487219-2) or 3xLP on Discogs and rip; a free full-comp stream/download also exists on the Internet Archive. Make sure you grab the original "Secrets", not the Visions remix.
