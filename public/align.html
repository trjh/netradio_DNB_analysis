/align — sample inspector

Verify and hand-tune one sync point at a time: stream fixed, original shiftable, live subtraction. All DSP runs server-side in the checkout's venv (band-limited at 16 kHz); this page draws, nudges (←/→ ±1 smp, shift=16), and plays (space combined · s subtraction · [/] speed). See docs/PLAN_align_tool.md §5.3. Whole-pair listening lives on the /align/review page (DP-22).
The align inspector's DSP is not configured on this server: it needs the checkout's .venv (make venv) and the originals (NETRADIO_SOURCES_DIR, else sources_local/). Hints from Pass 1 (streamalign match-hints) land in the labels dir and appear here automatically.
